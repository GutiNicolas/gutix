# T-01: Templates into gutix, with review tier and decisions

- **Roadmap:** ../ROADMAP.md · **Phase:** 1 · **Repo:** gutix · **Base branch:** master · **Branch:** feat/human-ownership-p1 (one session builds the phase, D6)
- **Depends on:** none · **Model:** main · **Principles:** P1, P2, P3, P4

## Goal
The feature templates are versioned in gutix and make every worker show what it decided, and every roadmap carry the
user's own hypothesis and definition of done.

## Scope
- Create `skills/feature-intake/templates/{ROADMAP,task,worker-brief}.md`, starting from `~/.claude/templates/feature/`.
- `task.md`: `**Review tier:** read | evidence` in the header; Result with the five headings of the roadmap's
  Contracts, in that order.
- `ROADMAP.md`: `## Hypothesis` and `## Definition of done` (the user's words) after Goal; the visible release line
  with `last signed off`.
- `worker-brief.md`: fill every Result heading; "none" only when true; a requirement reinterpreted because two clashed
  is a decision; `Where to look` points at what a human must read, not at everything.
- Templates are public (D12): the task's repo-rules criterion names the repo's `CLAUDE.md` and the standards it
  lists, not specific private skills.
- `skills/triage/SKILL.md`: point to `../feature-intake/templates/ROADMAP.md` instead of `~/.claude/templates/feature/`.
- Out of scope: feature-intake's own references (T-04), deleting the old folder and the global CLAUDE.md (T-07).

## Contract
ROADMAP.md → Contracts: task.md, ROADMAP.md template.
